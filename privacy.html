Privacy Policy

Channel Manager — last updated 9 October 2026

The short version: this tool only ever touches YouTube channels that I own. It does not collect viewer data, has no user accounts, and shares nothing with anyone.

1. Who runs this

Channel Manager is a private automation tool operated by its owner. Contact: hh1092838@gmail.com.

2. Data this tool accesses

3. What is never accessed or collected

  • No viewer personal data. The tool has no access to viewer names, emails, comments, or any personally identifiable information.
  • No third-party channels. It is scoped to channels I own and cannot read or modify any other channel.
  • No user data, because there are no users.
  • No tracking, analytics or advertising of any kind.
  • No data sold, rented, traded or shared with advertisers, brokers, data aggregators or any third party.

4. Where data is stored

  • Credentials and credentials-related files are stored on a private server I control, in files readable only by the server's user.
  • Content files are stored on that server and deleted on a retention schedule.
  • Published videos and the statistics associated with them are stored by YouTube on YouTube's own infrastructure, under YouTube's privacy policy.

5. Who it is shared with

Google, in exactly two ways: the OAuth token exchange with Google's authorisation servers, and API calls to YouTube. Beyond that, no data leaves the server I control.

6. How long data is kept

  • OAuth tokens are kept while the channel is active and are deleted when a channel is disconnected.
  • Uploaded and downloaded media files are deleted automatically on a retention schedule.
  • Analytics logs are kept for operational troubleshooting and then discarded.

7. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time by removing the app from myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revocation takes effect immediately. On request I will also delete the stored credentials for any channel.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date. Continued use after a change means the updated policy applies.
